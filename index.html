How to use it

No install · no account
Get a clean Japanese ROM

Super Robot Wars L for Nintendo DS, Japanese region, never patched before. This site does not provide or link to ROMs — you need your own copy.

Open the patcher and choose it

Press Patch ROM to open the patcher on its own page, then drag the file in or pick it from your device. Nothing is uploaded — the file is read on your own device and never leaves it.

Wait for the check

The page confirms the file is the exact ROM this patch supports. If it isn't, you get a specific reason and what to do, not a generic error.

Patch and download

Press Patch ROM. When it finishes, the file is verified and handed to you as a download. Keep it somewhere safe.

Questions

Is my ROM uploaded anywhere?
No. The file is read on your device by your browser and never leaves it. Nothing you open on this page is sent anywhere. Not your ROM, not the file you get back.
Which ROM do I need?
The Japanese release of Super Robot Wars L for Nintendo DS, unmodified. Other regions, other versions, or a ROM that has already been patched will be rejected with a specific message explaining why.
What does "in game" mean in the progress table?
Text can be translated and still not appear in the game. It has to be inserted and then tested. Translated is how much of the text is written. In game is how much has been inserted and verified inside a playable build. A gap between the two means the work exists but isn't shipped yet.
Something failed. What do I do?
Each failure shows a specific reason and the next step. Most come down to the wrong file, a broken download, or not enough free memory. If the same version keeps failing for you, report it with the code shown.
Do I need this site again after patching?
No. The patched file is complete and self-contained. Keep it. If you lose it, your original ROM can always be patched again with the same process.
Will my save still work?
Yes. In every test so far, an existing save has loaded normally, including saves made on the original Japanese ROM. The translation replaces text, not save structure.

Back it up anyway before you first load the patched ROM, and keep your clean Japanese ROM somewhere safe. If your emulator or flashcart pairs .nds and .sav by filename, give both files the same name.
Does this project distribute ROMs?
No. This project does not distribute ROMs. A pre-patched ROM is your own ROM, patched with our English translation. If a friend wants to play in English, send them here and they make their own.

What this is

This is an English translation patch of Super Robot Wars L for the Nintendo DS.

The translation patch is mine. The game is not. You bring your own copy of the Japanese game, and the page assembles an English version of it on your own device. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored. There is no account to make and no file to download. It doesn't give you the game, it just lets you read it in English.

It's free

Using it costs nothing. There is nothing to buy, nothing to subscribe to, and nothing to unlock.

How I'd like you to use it

Come to this page, patch the game you own, and play it. That's the intended path, and it keeps everything in one place where I can fix things and answer questions.

The patcher runs in your browser. When you close the tab, the file it made is gone. There is no download on this page. No file the patcher produces leaves your browser, and none is offered to anyone else.

About the patched game

A patched ROM is the game plus my translation. The game in it belongs to Bandai Namco; the English translation is the part I made. I claim nothing in the game, and I don't host or link to a patched ROM.
